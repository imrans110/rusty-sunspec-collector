# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
It applies to everyone taking part in the project: in issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews
and any other project space, and when representing the project elsewhere.

In short:

- Be respectful and considerate, including when you disagree. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Welcome newcomers and questions; assume good intent.
- No harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, sexualised language or imagery, or
  publishing other people's private information.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it privately to the maintainer,
[@imrans110](https://github.com/imrans110), using the contact details on that GitHub profile. Do
not report it in a public issue. Reports are handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

The maintainer reviews every report and may respond with a private warning, removal of comments
or contributions, or a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces, following the Contributor
Covenant's [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
